Our Alma Mater

Pragathi High School

📍 Centenary Colony, Peddapalli, Telangana

For decades, Pragathi High School has been a beacon of knowledge, character building, and community spirit in the heart of Peddapalli. Our school has nurtured countless young minds who have gone on to make a difference in every corner of India and beyond.

This alumni platform was initiated by the proud Class of 2009 with a single mission: to unite every Pragathi Champ, celebrate our shared journey, and give back to the institution that shaped us all.

  • Networking & Professional Opportunities
  • Career Mentorship Programs
  • Annual Reunion Events
  • Scholarship & Community Initiatives
  • Campus Development Contributions
"To build a thriving, supportive alumni community that honours the legacy of Pragathi High School — fostering meaningful connections, celebrating achievements, and empowering every Pragathi Champ to reach their fullest potential."
